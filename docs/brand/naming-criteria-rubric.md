## 1. Naming Objectives

To establish a permanent, distinctive, and legally unencumbered product identity replacing the internal placeholder codename `DB Pro`.

---

## 2. Hard Rejection Rules (Disqualifiers)

A candidate is **immediately disqualified** if it violates any of the following:

1. **Exact/Confusing Trademark Collision**: Direct collision with existing database or developer software (e.g. `DataGrip`, `TablePlus`, `Beekeeper`, `Postico`, `DBeaver`, `Supabase`, `Neon`, `dbpro.app`).
2. **Generic Keyword Soup**: Combinations like `DBPro`, `SQLStudio`, `DatabaseAI`, `SQLCopilot`.
3. **Misleading Constraint**: Names implying SQL-only, Postgres-only, or AI-only (e.g., `OnlyPostgres`, `SQLBot`, `PureSQL`).
4. **Pronunciation / Awkwardness**: Names hard to pronounce or with negative/inappropriate slang connotations in English, Vietnamese, or major European languages.
5. **SEO & Search Dead-end**: Keywords overwhelmed by billion-dollar generic terms or medical/tax acronyms.

## 3. Scoring Dimensions (Weighted 100-Point Scale)

| Dimension | Weight | Description | Scoring Guide |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Memorability & Distinctiveness** | 25 pts | Stands out, punchy, clean syllable structure | 20-25: unique & sharp; 10-19: decent; <10: forgettable |
| **Positioning Fit** | 25 pts | Conveys precision, data engineering, native speed, intelligence | 20-25: natural fit; 10-19: acceptable; <10: misaligned |
| **Global Pronounceability** | 15 pts | Easy to say/spell across EN, VI, and international dev teams | 12-15: flawless; 8-11: minor friction; <8: tongue-twister |
| **Extensibility** | 15 pts | Scales from desktop DB IDE to cross-database Action/Agent platform | 12-15: broad horizon; 8-11: moderate; <8: narrow niche |
| **Domain & Handle Viability** | 20 pts | `.com`/`.dev`/`.io`/`get<name>.com` and GitHub/CLI availability | 15-20: clean path; 10-14: moderate; <10: squatted/costly |

---

## 4. Evaluation Rubric & Thresholds

* **Score >= 80**: Eligible for Shortlist & Live Collision Diligence (#99 / #100).
* **Score 65-79**: Backup candidate.
* **Score < 65**: Rejected.
